STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE — HOT WORK SAFETY (INTERNAL / CONFIDENTIAL)
SOP-HW-014

1. PERMIT
   - A hot work permit is mandatory before any welding, grinding, or cutting.
   - Permit valid for a maximum of 8 hours per shift.

2. PRECAUTIONS
   - Keep a 10 meter clearance of flammable material around the work area.
   - A trained fire watch must be present during work and for 30 minutes after.
   - Minimum one 9 kg DCP fire extinguisher at the work site.

3. GAS TESTING
   - Test atmosphere for flammable gas before starting.
   - LEL must read below 5 percent to proceed.

4. EMERGENCY
   - Stop work immediately if LEL exceeds 10 percent.
   - Evacuate and inform the shift supervisor and control room.

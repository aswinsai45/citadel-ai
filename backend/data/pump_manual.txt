CENTRIFUGAL PUMP — MODEL CP-200
OPERATIONS & MAINTENANCE MANUAL (INTERNAL / CONFIDENTIAL)

1. LUBRICATION
   - Bearing lubrication: Use ISO VG 68 mineral oil.
   - Oil change interval: every 4000 operating hours or 6 months.
   - Oil level must stay between the MIN and MAX marks on the sight glass.

2. OPERATING LIMITS
   - Max discharge pressure: 16 bar
   - Max fluid temperature: 120 C
   - Rated flow: 200 m3/h at 2950 rpm

3. STARTUP CHECKLIST
   - Confirm suction valve fully open.
   - Prime the casing; do not run dry.
   - Verify rotation direction matches arrow on casing.

4. COMMON FAULTS
   - Excessive vibration: check coupling alignment and bearing wear.
   - Overheating: check oil level and cooling water flow.
